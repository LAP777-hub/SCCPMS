## POPIA Compliance (South Africa)

### POPIA Status

SCCPMS is designed to be POPIA (Protection of Personal Information Act) compliant for South African healthcare environments. Key compliance features include:

- **Lawful Processing**: Process data fairly and lawfully with user consent
- **Purpose Limitation**: Use data only for stated medical purposes
- **Data Minimization**: Collect only necessary health information
- **Security Safeguards**: Appropriate security measures to protect personal information
- **Data Subject Rights**: Users can access, correct, and delete their data
- **Cross-Border Transfers**: Appropriate safeguards for international data transfers

### Special Personal Information (Health Data)

Health information is classified as "special personal information" under POPIA and requires:
- Explicit consent for processing
- Enhanced security measures
- Strict access controls
- Breach notification to Information Regulator within 72 hours
- Retention only as long as necessary

### Data Residency

Note: Patient profiles may be stored in Supabase's cloud infrastructure, while clinical records are stored in your local PostgreSQL database. Ensure this arrangement complies with POPIA's cross-border transfer requirements. Consult with legal counsel to verify data residency compliance.

### POPIA Training

All staff with access to personal information receive POPIA training upon hiring and annually thereafter.
